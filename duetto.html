Welcome to Duetto

Real-time piano lessons that actually feel real-time. Here's how it works:

  1. Plug in your USB-MIDI keyboard before opening this page (browsers detect MIDI on load).
  2. Allow camera and microphone when prompted.
  3. You'll get a short ID — share it with your partner. Or paste theirs and Connect.
  4. Both of you play freely. Your notes light up in cyan, theirs in magenta.

Works best in Chrome or Edge on a wired internet connection. Web MIDI is not supported in Safari or Firefox.
